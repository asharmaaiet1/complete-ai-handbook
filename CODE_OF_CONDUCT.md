# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Complete AI Handbook is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Welcome questions and beginner contributions.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and reasoning.
- Give credit to original work.
- Respect privacy and intellectual property.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Plagiarism or knowingly misrepresenting another person's work.

## Scope

This policy applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-related community interactions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. Reports may be sent privately to the repository owner or designated maintainers. Maintainers will review reports fairly and may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary.

This document is adapted from common open-source community conduct practices. A project owner should add a monitored contact route before inviting broad community participation.
