## Release conventions

- **Added:** new learning content or functionality.
- **Changed:** updates to existing material.
- **Fixed:** corrections to errors or broken examples.
- **Deprecated:** material that should no longer be followed.
- **Reviewed:** content checked against current references and tested versions.
